Common questions

My step count shows zero

Just Steps reads your steps from Apple Health, so it needs permission to do that. Open the Health app on your iPhone, tap your profile picture, and check that step access is turned on for Just Steps. The app itself will offer this hint whenever your count sits at zero.

I cannot find the complication on my watch face

Press and hold your watch face, tap Edit, swipe to Complications, and choose a slot. The app is listed under J for Just Steps.

If it does not appear in the list at all, restart your watch. watchOS sometimes needs a restart before a newly installed app shows up there.

My complication is not updating

watchOS decides how often complications refresh, and it does so less frequently to save battery. Opening the app updates it immediately.

I am not getting notifications

Check both switches on the third page of the app, and make sure notifications are allowed for Just Steps in the Watch app on your iPhone. The goal notification only fires between 9am and 9pm, and the evening reminder only arrives at 7pm on days you are still short of your goal.

Why do older days say "No goal data"?

Apple Health has been recording your steps since long before you installed this app, so your history goes back further than the app does. For days from before you installed it, there is no goal on record, and the app says so rather than judging those days against a goal you never set. Every day from your first launch onwards keeps the goal that was actually in force.

Does it need my iPhone?

No. Just Steps runs perfectly fine without ever opening the phone app.
